Publish your changes

Members get new news, classes, programmes and challenges the next time they open the app online. During the pilot, publishing means sending the club file to your app manager, who puts it live, usually the same day.

1. Check it on this device

Shows your draft in the member app on this computer only, marked “Coach preview”.

2. Send it live

Download club.json and send it to your app manager. It includes any invites you turned off.

Start again from the live version

Throws away your draft and reloads what members currently see.
